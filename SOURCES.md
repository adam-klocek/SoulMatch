# Sources and measurement notes

Retrieved 16 October 2026.

- User-supplied Interests, Hobbies & Lifestyle Questionnaire prompt: the source for all 16 interest categories, 195 options, seven lifestyle questions, 12 default shared activities, and the separation of enjoyment/frequency/partner importance. No external personal data was used.
- IPIP official factor-marker key: https://ipip.ori.org/newBigFive5broadKey.htm
- IPIP permission (public domain): https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm
- IPIP citation guidance: https://ipip.ori.org/newCitation.htm
- Goldberg, L. R. (1992). The development of markers for the Big-Five factor structure. Psychological Assessment, 4, 26–42. https://doi.org/10.1037/1040-3590.4.1.26
- Soto, C. J., & John, O. P. (2017). Short and extra-short forms of the Big Five Inventory–2: The BFI-2-S and BFI-2-XS. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrp.2017.02.004 — reference for the inactive original-version adapters; no BFI-2 item text is bundled.
- Brněnský dotazník výšky (zkrácená verze): https://fssvm6.fss.muni.cz/vyska/
- Height questionnaire source and GPL-3.0 license: https://github.com/hynekcigler/Height-questionnaire — source copies in `third_party/height/`.
- Original height app: ©2019 Hynek Cígler, Department of Psychology, Faculty of Social Studies, Masaryk University. Original items/data: Karel Rečka (2018); short-form selection: Martin Tancoš (2019). The English translation and modern Shiny wrapper are adaptations made for this project in 2026. Core sum/regression/interval formulas are retained. The reference groups are not selected from gender identity; raw scores are not used for between-group matching.
- Posit file-input documentation: https://shiny.posit.co/r/components/inputs/file-input/

The active personality questionnaire is a **custom experimental ten-item IPIP selection**, two per domain; it is not the BFI-2, TIPI or an established validated abbreviated instrument. No norms or percentiles are supplied. The three additional reflections are original exploratory tools. The original Czech height tool is educational; the English adaptation and its use in dating have not been validated. No source establishes that SoulMatch's scores predict romantic or friendship outcomes.

The combined software distribution is GPL-3.0 because it includes the height adaptation. Original GPL code/license and the original project's MIT notice are retained. Generated demo portraits depict fictional adults; provenance/prompt are in `design/ASSETS.md`.
