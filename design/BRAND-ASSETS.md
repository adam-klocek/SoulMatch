# SoulMatch — The First Spark

Original matchstick artwork: one gently tilted deep-teal stem, an asymmetric muted-coral flame, and a soft-peach heart suggestion inside the flame. The heart is an accent rather than the silhouette. No gradients, raster images, external links, fonts, filters, or scripts occur in the shipped logos. All logo backgrounds are transparent.

The wordmark uses outlined Nunito Sans 12pt SemiBold, a rounded sans serif. Outlines make it independent of font installation and browser downloads. Original font metadata: Copyright 2016 The Nunito Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/Fonthausen/NunitoSans)

Primary horizontal and monochrome wordmarks should be at least 135 CSS pixels wide. Use the standalone icon at 22 pixels high or larger; favicon has a square optical frame. On deep teal use the dark variation: ivory wordmark and stem with coral/peach flame. Preserve empty space of at least half the flame width. Do not stretch, recolor individual letters, add shadows to the asset, or put the light-background monochrome on teal.

Optional flicker is off by default, applies only to the initial loading flame when explicitly enabled by SOULMATCH_FLAME_ANIMATION=true, and stops after two slow cycles. Reduced-motion disables it. All other occurrences stay static.

Files in www/brand/: soulmatch-logo-primary.svg, soulmatch-icon.svg, soulmatch-logo-dark.svg, soulmatch-logo-monochrome.svg, soulmatch-icon-monochrome.svg, soulmatch-favicon.svg. Legacy interlaced-loop assets are preserved but no longer used by the application.
